白い熊 画 is a Japanese OCR popup dictionary that stays on top of all your apps: move a capture box over any Japanese text — raw manga, untranslated games, anything on screen — and it recognises the characters with optical character recognition (OCR) and looks them up, without switching apps.

A fork of Kaku by 0xbad1d3a5, continued where it stopped in 2022: a modern build for current Android, free of Google services and ads, with no network access at all.
